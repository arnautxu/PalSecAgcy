ReferenceOS

Privacy Policy

ReferenceOS is a local-first Safari extension for saving and retrieving visual web references. Your library is private by default.

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Who is responsible

ReferenceOS is provided by PALSEC AGCY. For privacy questions or requests, contact arnau@palsec.agency.

Information ReferenceOS processes

ReferenceOS only processes information needed to build your private reference library. When you choose to save a page, this may include:

  • the page URL, title, domain, description, icon and open-graph image;
  • the text you select, and a short page excerpt used to make saved references searchable;
  • an optional visual screenshot of the page, when you keep that option enabled;
  • your notes, tags, projects, categories and saved-reference dates.

ReferenceOS does not collect your browsing history. It accesses page information only for the active page or selection you explicitly save.

Where your information is stored

Your library is stored locally on your Mac, using Safari extension storage and the ReferenceOS app’s local storage. You can export or delete your saved references from the Library.

Private iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, your library is synced only through your private iCloud/CloudKit database under your Apple ID. ReferenceOS does not operate a server that receives or stores your library.

Optional on-device assistance

ReferenceOS can use Apple’s on-device intelligence features to help categorize or search your saved references. This processing happens on your device. It is not required to use the Library.

Optional X bookmark import

If you explicitly connect an X account, ReferenceOS sends your OAuth credentials to X only to obtain your X bookmarks and account name. The credentials are stored in your Mac’s Keychain, not in the ReferenceOS library or on a ReferenceOS server. Imported bookmark content is then stored in your local library. X’s own privacy terms apply to its service.

What we do not do

ReferenceOS does not sell personal information, use advertising networks, run third-party analytics or trackers, or create a ReferenceOS user account. We do not use your saved references to build advertising profiles.

Your choices

You control the references you save and can edit or delete them at any time. You can disable private iCloud sync, disconnect X bookmark import, and remove the extension. If you need help with a privacy request, email us at arnau@palsec.agency.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ReferenceOS changes. The effective date above will show when it was last revised.
